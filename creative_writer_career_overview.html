Duties & Authoring Workflow Pipeline

The structured 6-stage lifecycle for novels, screenplays, and interactive video game stories.

01

Research, Premise & World Bible

Defining the logline premise, conducting historical/technical research, compiling the world bible, magic systems, and faction histories.

World Bible & Logline
02

Beat Sheet & Narrative Outlining

Plotting Save-the-Cat or 3-act beat sheets, mapping chapter scenes, establishing character motivations, and designing branching choice trees.

Outlines & Choice Nodes
03

First Draft Manuscript Execution

Writing raw chapter text or script scenes, maintaining daily word count targets, capturing dialogue momentum, and drafting non-stop.

Raw Word Count Pass
04

Structural & Line Editing Passes

Analyzing plot holes, trimming fluff, tightening sentence rhythm, polishing character voices, and verifying consistency across lore.

Structural Polish & Line Edits
05

Beta Readers & Sensitivity Review

Distributing manuscript to beta readers, incorporating editor feedback notes, adjusting emotional beats, and proofreading punctuation.

Beta Feedback & Proofing
06

Pitch Package / Game Engine Handoff

Preparing agent queries and synopsis pitches, or exporting Twine/Articy JSON node files for unity/unreal engine developer integration.

Query Pitch or Engine Export
